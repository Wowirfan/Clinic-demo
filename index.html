Science-Informed Care

Vampire Facial
(PRP Therapy)

A PRP-based facial treatment performed after professional assessment and consultation.

Support Your Skin's Natural Glow

PRP treatments use a patient's own blood components and are performed by qualified professionals according to individual suitability.

✓Supports skin texture and radiance
✓May support collagen-related skin renewal
✓Personalized treatment approach
✓Professional aftercare guidance
01

Blood Collection

A small blood sample is collected by a trained professional.

02

PRP Separation

The sample is processed to separate platelet-rich plasma.

03

Preparation

The prepared PRP is readied for the selected treatment method.

04

Application

PRP is applied using the technique selected by the clinician.

05

Aftercare

You receive personalized post-treatment instructions.
